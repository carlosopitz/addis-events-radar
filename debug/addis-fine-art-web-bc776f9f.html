  • Amel Bashier

    Solo Exhibition

    12 September 2026 – 10 January 2027
    Museum für Moderne Kunst (MMK), Zollamt
    Frankfurt, Germany

    ‘Essence of Life’ is Amel Bashier's first institutional solo exhibition, for which she has created numerous new works.

    The works of Sudanese artist Amel Bashier (b. 1981) celebrate women globally, granting them the space and respect they merit. Her female subjects are depicted individually, in trios, and in larger groups, at work or at home, shown as radiant, strong, and visionary as they are lonely, tender, and fragile. Bashier's works evoke Nubian, Assyrian as well as Indian figural forms with their big hands and feet, vibrant contrasting colors, symmetry, and a two-dimensional quality that lends her contemporary themes the historical significance they deserve.

  • Tizta Berhanu

    29 April – 1 June 2027
    Palais de Tokyo
    Paris, France

    The Toyota Tsusho CFAO African Art Award recognises outstanding contemporary artists across Africa and aims to support the next generation of artistic talent on the continent. Selected from a group of nearly 100 nominated artists by an international panel of curators and critics, Tizta is among five award recipients this year. Works by the winners will be presented in a series of exhibitions beginning in Togo in June 2026, followed by presentations in Japan and France.

  • Merikokeb Berhanu

    Solo Exhibition

    9 June – 14 August 2026
    Esther Schipper
    Seoul, South Korea

    Cellular Memory is Merikokeb Berhanu’s first solo presentation in Asia, and her second with Esther Schipper. On view are seven new paintings which explore the tension between the natural and the technological through the artist’s semi-abstracted figurative language.
