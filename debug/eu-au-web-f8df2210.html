EU and African partners call for responsible AI and protected journalists

The European Union Delegation to the African Union supported the Federation of African Journalists (FAJ)’s event “Advancing Responsible Artificial Intelligence and Decent Work for Quality Journalism in Africa” held in Addis Ababa on 24 and 25 October 2026, in collaboration with UNESCO, the International Labour Organization (ILO) and African Union Commission.
