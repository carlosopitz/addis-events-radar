The Italian Cultural Institute of Addis Ababa holds a public auction for the sale of the following service car, according to the procedures specified in the Notice below.

– Car: Service Car (Diplomatic Plate);
– Type: Toyota Land Cruiser (105) Gx10;
– Duty Free;
– Year and Country of Manufacture: 2002, Japan;
– Km.: 225,622;
– Base Price: ETB 2,500,000.00.

The car will be assigned, in its current state, by public auction with secret bids and award to the highest bidder. The car and its documents may be inspected from Monday to Friday (from 9:00 to 16:00), by appointment to be requested via email at iicaddisabeba@esteri.it or by calling the numbers: +251 111553427 / 111573080 / 911122221.
Bids (for an amount not less than ETB 2,500,000.00) must be received by the Italian Cultural Institute of Addis Ababa (Belay Zelleke Street, No. 9) no later than 16:00 (GMT +3) on 4 February 2026, in a sealed envelope marked “Do not open – Bid for the purchase of service car”.
