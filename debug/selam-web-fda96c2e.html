Selam Ethiopia is a catalyst for cultural transformation. From vibrant festivals to empowering capacity-building initiatives, we breathe life into Ethiopia’s rich traditions. Our commitment reaches beyond the arts, empowering individuals, supporting job creation, defending artistic freedom, championing human rights and democracy, and fostering lasting peace. As an international organisation, we unite cultures, crossing borders with creativity. Selam Ethiopia stands strong today, actively generating opportunities, advocating for artistic freedom, and amplifying voices for peace.
