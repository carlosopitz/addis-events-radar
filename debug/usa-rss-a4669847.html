Security Alert – U.S. Embassy Addis Ababa, Ethiopia (October 28, 2026)

Location: Ethiopia

Event:  The U.S. Embassy continues to monitor events in northern Ethiopia. Fighting in Tigray appears to be concentrated in areas outside Mekelle and near the border with Eritrea. The embassy also continues to monitor ongoing fighting in Moyale, the primary border with Kenya, shared by both the Oromia and Somali regions, which appears to have calmed. The overall security situation may change at any time.

Flights remain suspended at the airports in Axum and Shire. Contact the airline directly to book tickets and for notifications on operations.

U.S. citizens are reminded to reconsider travel to Ethiopia. U.S. citizens are advised not to travel to the Amhara Region, the Tigray Region, or the Afar Region (particularly northern Afar) for any reason. U.S. citizens in those areas should leave if it is safe to do so.

Some social media accounts contain false and intentionally misleading reports about the security situation in Ethiopia. U.S. citizens are urged to verify information using multiple sources of reliable information.

Review the State Department Travel Advisory for Ethiopia and enroll in Smart Traveler Enrollment Program (STEP) for more information.

Actions to take:

  • Enroll in Smart Traveler Enrollment Program (STEP) to receive security and health updates.
  • Avoid Ethiopian military installations.
  • Do not travel to Amhara (including Lalibela), Tigray, or Afar.
  • Monitor multiple, reliable local news sources for security updates.
  • Review your personal security plans.

Secretary Rubio’s Call with Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy

Readout

Office of the Spokesman

October 14, 2026

Secretary of State Marco Rubio spoke today with Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed Ali. The Secretary and Prime Minister discussed heightened tensions with Eritrea.

The Secretary supports de-escalation by all sides. The only sustainable solution to internal and external tensions is achieved through dialogue and engagement as agreed to in the 2018 Ethiopia-Eritrea peace framework.

The Secretary stressed that all countries in the Horn of Africa must respect the sovereignty and territorial integrity of their neighbors and avoid cross-border incursions. The Secretary emphasized that the region cannot tolerate more conflict.

Security Alert: The U.S. Embassy continues to monitor events in Northern Ethiopia (Oct 08, 2026)

Location: Ethiopia

Event:  The U.S. Embassy continues to monitor events in Northern Ethiopia. The security situation conditions may change at any time, though the conflict appears to be concentrated to two areas: north of Adigrat and north of Shire. Telecommunications have resumed in Mekelle and much of the Tigray region Mobile banking services and public electricity utilities have partially resumed. All remain subject to interruption without notice. U.S. citizens should notify friends and family of their status while communications networks are active.

The U.S. Embassy has received no further reports of the use of long-range weapons since the Security Alert sent on October 7.

U.S. citizens in the Amhara Region (including Lalibela), the Tigray Region, and the Afar Region should leave if it is safe to do so or shelter in place until conditions improve. Flights remain suspended at the airports in Mekelle, Axum, and Shire. Stay in touch with the airline for notifications on operations.

Some social media accounts contain false and misleading reports about the security situation in Ethiopia. U.S. citizens are urged to verify information using multiple sources or reliable information. Review the State Department Travel Advisory for Ethiopia and enroll in Smart Traveler Enrollment Program (STEP) for more information.

Actions to take:

  • Avoid Ethiopian military installations.
  • Do not travel to Amhara (including Lalibela), Tigray, or Afar.
  • Monitor multiple, reliable local news sources for security updates.
  • Review your personal security plans.

Security Alert: U.S. Embassy Addis Ababa, Ethiopia (October 7, 2026)

Location: Ethiopia

Event: The U.S. Embassy in Addis Ababa is aware of reports of possible long-range attacks directed at Ethiopian military installations on several instances in October of this year. U.S. citizens should accordingly exercise caution and follow the advice of local authorities.

U.S. citizens are advised not to travel to the Amhara Region (including Lalibela), the Tigray Region, or the Afar Region for any reason. U.S. citizens in those areas are advised to leave if it is safe to do so or shelter in place until conditions improve, monitor local media for updates, and stay in touch with their airline for notifications on operations. Internet and phone connectivity could be interrupted at any time.

At this time, U.S. citizens are reminded to reconsider travel to Ethiopia. While the security situation in Addis Ababa remains stable, there is sporadic armed conflict and civil unrest in Northern Ethiopia and the situation there remains fluid. Disinformation about the security situation in Ethiopia is rampant, particularly on social media, therefore U.S. citizens are urged to follow reliable news outlets for the most updated information.

Review the State Department Travel Advisory for Ethiopia and enroll in Smart Traveler Enrollment Program (STEP) for more information.

Actions to take:

  • Avoid Ethiopian military installations
  • Do not travel to Amhara (including Lalibela), Tigray, or Afar.
  • Monitor reliable local media for security updates.
  • Review your personal security plans.
